Specialty Capabilities: Hard Gold & Selective Gold PCB

For applications requiring gold plating on connectors, edge fingers, and high-cycle contact pads, HT Global Circuits offers Type I, Type II, and Type III hard gold plating. Our selective gold PCB process ensures hard gold is applied only where needed (avoiding solderability issues), with thickness options from 10 to 50 micro-inches. Whether you need a hard gold PCB for aerospace connectors or selective gold for memory modules, our engineering team delivers IPC Class 2/3 quality
